# Library (Stauffer): Walkthrough

Spoilers. You have 15:00. The Dean comes with 10:00 and 5:00 left: click the glowing hiding spot
(carrel, computer desks, or circulation desk) within 12 seconds. `H` gives a free hint at any time.

## Floor 3: Silent Study

1. Click the **Lost and found box**. You get the Flashlight.
2. Click the Flashlight in your items, then click the **Whiteboard**. It shows `3 _ 1 _`.
3. Click the **Bookshelf**. You get a Toonie and a note that says `_ 7 _ 9`.
4. Click the **Stairwell door** and enter **3719**.
5. Click the door again to go down.

## Floor 2: Computer Commons

1. Use the **Toonie** on the **Vending machine**. You get a Screwdriver.
2. Use the **Screwdriver** on the **Electrical panel**. The power comes back.
3. The **Brass plaque** says 1841 and the **Sticky note** says the password is the charter year.
4. Click the **Staff computer** and enter **1841**.
5. Click the **Stairwell door** to go down.

## Ground Floor: Circulation Hall

1. Click the **Staff noticeboard**. You get the Magnet on a string.
2. Use it on the **Book return**. You get a Small key.
3. Use the Small key on the **Circulation desk**. You get a Keycard.
4. Use the Keycard on the **Main doors**. You're out.
